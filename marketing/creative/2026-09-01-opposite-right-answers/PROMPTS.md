Mode: built-in image generation
Use case: `ads-marketing`

## 1. Opening image

```text
Asset type: TikTok photo carousel, portrait 9:16
Primary request: Create the opening image for a subtle story about two NYC renters touring the same apartment and wanting opposite things.
Scene/backdrop: a real small prewar NYC one-bedroom during an open house, worn hardwood floor, modest white kitchen with a clearly visible dishwasher, one narrow window with limited natural light, authentic rental imperfections, not luxury real estate.
Subject: two unrelated renters in their late 20s touring at the same time. One renter is quietly pleased by the usable floor space and dishwasher. The other renter stands closer to the narrow window and looks thoughtful, slightly concerned by the weak natural light. Their reactions should be subtle and believable, not theatrical.
Style/medium: photorealistic natural editorial photography, candid documentary feel, realistic skin and fabric texture, understated New York atmosphere.
Composition/framing: vertical 9:16, wide enough to understand the apartment and both reactions, strong top third and upper-middle negative space for native TikTok text overlay, faces not covered by the future text area.
Lighting/mood: late-afternoon winter daylight, calm, observant, quietly tense.
Color palette: restrained navy, warm wood, off-white, muted green accents.
Constraints: no in-image text, no logos, no watermark, no visible real-estate brand, no exaggerated expressions, no luxury staging, no skyline cliché, no fisheye distortion.
```
