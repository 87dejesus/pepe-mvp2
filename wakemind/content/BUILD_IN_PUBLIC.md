# Build in public

The validation strategy is not paid acquisition. The founder documents the build
publicly — one short-form video per day — which produces the cohort, the
verbatim and the demand signal at the same time.

## Why this is also a research instrument

Comments are the verbatim bank. Everything usable lands in
`docs/product/discovery/VERBATIM_BANK.md` with a source, because the ADHD and
alarm-app quotes gathered during discovery were collected through search
aggregation and still need verification at source before any of them go into
marketing.

## Cadence

One video per day. Real struggles included — the Windows/Mac blocker, the spike
failing, a missed alarm. The build-in-public audience rewards the failures more
reliably than the wins, and the failures are cheaper to film.

## What we do not post

- Anyone's calendar content, including the founder's, unblurred
- Anyone's voice promise without explicit permission
- Tester data of any kind, aggregated or not
- Claims about efficacy before H1 answers. **Not one.** The category is full of
  products asserting outcomes they never measured; the entire differentiation
  here is that we actually measure. Spending that on early hype is a bad trade.

## Recruiting the cohort

The 30–60 testers come from this audience. Screen for iOS 26 (ADR-0003) and
explain the study honestly, including the 30% of mornings that deliberately do
nothing (ADR-0010).
