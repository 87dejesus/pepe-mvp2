# Privacy Policy (DRAFT — not legal advice, review before publishing)

WakeMind is built so that there is almost nothing to disclose.

**What stays on your device, always:** your calendar events, the promises you
write, and any voice recording you make. These are never uploaded, never
transmitted, and never readable by us.

**What we collect, only if you agree:** anonymous usage events — how many times
an alarm was snoozed, how long a screen took to complete, whether you confirmed
being up. No text, no audio, no event titles, no name, no email, no location.
Tied to a random identifier that is not linked to you and disappears if you
delete the app.

**Why:** WakeMind is being validated as a research build. The events above are
how we learn whether it works. You can decline and use the app fully.

**Retention:** promises 30 days; audio deleted after the morning it belongs to;
behavioural events 90 days.

**Your choices:** revoke calendar or microphone access in iOS Settings at any
time; turn off study data in the app; delete everything from Settings.
