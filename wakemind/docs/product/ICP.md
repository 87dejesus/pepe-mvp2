# ICP

Derived from research, not intuition. Full reasoning in
`discovery/DISCOVERY_ROUND_1.md` §5.

## Primary — adults 25–40 with ADHD

The clinical literature describes our problem in our own terms: the transition
from sleep to wakefulness demands peak executive function at the exact moment
those systems are offline. Combined with time blindness, this group needs no
convincing that the problem exists.

They also already pay for external scaffolding, self-identify publicly, cluster
in findable communities, and treat "I forgot I had a thing today" as a
diagnostic fact rather than an embarrassment — which means they talk about it,
which is the growth engine.

## Secondary — meeting-dense knowledge workers with morning anxiety

Calendar density makes the product non-empty. Anxiety extends sleep inertia by
roughly 14 minutes. Highest willingness to pay.

## Not our user

| Segment | Why not |
|---|---|
| Students | Broke, and their snoozing is sleep debt. Biology, not automaticity. |
| Heavy sleepers who want volume | Alarmy owns them. Our product is quiet. |
| Sparse-calendar users | The product thins out. H6 tests whether the promise carries it. |

## Screening the validation cohort

iOS 26 (ADR-0003) · snoozes most mornings · has a calendar with something in it
on most weekdays · willing to be asked two questions over three weeks.
