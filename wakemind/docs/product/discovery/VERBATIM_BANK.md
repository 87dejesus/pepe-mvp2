## Notable absence

Discovery found abundant language about grogginess, oversleeping and guilt — and
almost none about "I dismissed my alarm without processing my day." The original
framing was a mechanism, not a felt problem. **Marketing speaks in the language
of this table, never in the language of the mechanism.**
