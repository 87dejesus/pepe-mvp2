# Content style guide

UI copy is English. Short, plain, adult. No exclamation marks in the morning.

## The tone rule (ADR-0009)

**Shame is a marketing register and never a product register.**

Marketing may name the moral hangover — *"You didn't decide to go back to sleep.
You just did it again."* That sentence sells the install because it is
recognition.

The product may never administer it. Guilt increases the behaviour we sell
ourselves as fixing.

## Banned in-product

- Snooze counters, streak counts, failure tallies
- "You broke your streak", "You've done this 4 days in a row"
- Comparisons to the user's past self
- Disappointed voice of any kind: "Again?", "Really?"
- Sarcasm, nagging, mock-authority
- Motivational-poster voice: "Crush your day", "No excuses"

## Required patterns

| Moment | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Night step | quiet, low-stakes | "What are you getting up for?" |
| Deadline | factual, not urgent | "Up by 7:20 to get there calm." |
| Interruption | neutral, no praise | "Which one did you write?" |
| Confirmed up | brief, then get out of the way | "That's it. See you tonight." |
| After a failed morning | absolving, forward-facing | "Yesterday's done. Here's tomorrow." |
| After an escape hatch | no comment at all | *(silence — it is logged, not judged)* |

## Voice

Steady, not cheerful. We are the thing that holds the promise, not the thing
that congratulates you for keeping it.
