# Design principles

The user is at their least capable in the exact moment we ask for something.
Every decision follows from that.

1. **One thing on screen at the moment of interruption.** Sustained attention is
   the most impaired faculty at wake; competing elements cost more than they do
   at any other hour.
2. **Recognition, never generation.** Show what they wrote. Never ask them to
   produce, prioritise or decide.
3. **The morning should feel smaller, not bigger.** Avoidant snoozing is real;
   an app that presents the day as a wall gives people a reason to hide.
4. **Typography carries the load.** Large, high-contrast, legible without
   glasses, at arm's length, in the dark, with one eye open.
5. **No decoration in the alarm path.** Animation that delays the promise by
   400ms is a bug.
6. **Failure resolves forward.** Never a tally, never a comparison to their past
   self (ADR-0009).
7. **The escape hatch is visible and unashamed.** A commitment device the user
   cannot escape is a trap and will be reviewed as one.
